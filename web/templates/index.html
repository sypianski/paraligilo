Important: Both files must be the same book — original and its translation. Two different books by the same author will produce confident-looking nonsense.
How the two modes differ

Deterministic mode uses surface tokens (years, numbers, rare proper-noun co-occurrences) as anchors, then a classic Gale-Church length-only dynamic-programming alignment. Free. Offline. Fast. Quality depends on how faithful the translation is structurally — classic literary translations score very high; heavily restructured modern ones score lower.

AI mode uses OpenAI multilingual embeddings to score semantic similarity between sentences, plus optional gpt-4o-mini to honestly label suspected non-alignments. Higher precision on hard cases (paraphrases, idioms, partial overlaps), and gives meaningful per-pair confidence. Costs ~$0.10/book paid to OpenAI.

What you'll get

alignment.jsonl — one record per aligned sentence pair (m:n moves supported), with confidence and method.

sample.md — 100 stratified examples for manual quality check.
